# Privacy Policy for Spoiler Stopper

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Spoiler Stopper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The extension reads video titles, links, channel names, and durations displayed on YouTube solely to identify and obscure potential sports spoilers. This processing happens locally in the browser. The extension does not send YouTube activity or page content to a remote server.

Spoiler Stopper stores one setting in Chrome's local extension storage: whether spoiler protection is enabled or disabled. This setting remains on the user's device.

Spoiler Stopper does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, remote code, or third-party data processors.

Spoiler Stopper's use of information received from Google services adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the extension's Chrome Web Store support page.
